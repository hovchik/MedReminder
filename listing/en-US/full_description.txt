MedReminder is the privacy-first medication reminder app that helps you and your family stay on track with prescriptions, vitamins, and supplements. Set up your medications in seconds — scan a label with your camera or let on-device AI auto-fill the details — and receive reliable, on-time reminders that even wake your screen.

🔔 SMART REMINDERS
• Exact-time alarms with full-screen alerts — never sleep through a dose
• Flexible schedules: daily, specific days, every X days, or every X hours
• Snooze, mark taken, or skip right from the notification
• Automatic rescheduling after reboot

💊 EASY MEDICATION SETUP
• Scan medication labels or barcodes with your camera (OCR & barcode support)
• AI auto-fill: enter just the name and AI suggests dosage, form, and instructions
• Track pills, capsules, liquids, injections, patches, sprays, drops, inhalers, and creams
• Stock tracking with low-supply refill reminders

👨‍👩‍👧 FAMILY & CAREGIVER SUPPORT
• Add family members and manage their medications too
• Caregiver notifications via SMS when doses are taken, missed, or cancelled
• Emergency mode: missed critical medications trigger an SMS with your live location
• Automatic phone call fallback when SMS delivery fails

🤖 AI-POWERED INSIGHTS
• On-device AI keeps your health data 100 % private
• Daily and weekly adherence analysis with personalized recommendations
• Deep medication analysis: interactions, side effects, body targets, and more
• Choose from on-device AI, cloud AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek), or auto

📊 STATS & HISTORY
• Visual adherence tracking — daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly
• Current streak counter to keep you motivated
• Full dose history: taken, missed, snoozed, and cancelled
• Export your data as JSON for backup or doctor visits

🔒 PRIVACY BY DESIGN
• All data stored locally on your device — no cloud sync, no accounts
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Camera images processed on-device only
• Cloud AI is optional and off by default

🌍 MULTILINGUAL
Available in English, Spanish, Russian, Armenian, Hindi, Japanese, Chinese, and Farsi.

Whether you take one daily vitamin or manage a complex regimen for the whole family, MedReminder is designed to be simple, reliable, and respectful of your privacy. Download it today and never miss a dose again.
